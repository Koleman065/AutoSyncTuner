# AutoSync Tuner – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 13, 2026_

AutoSync Tuner is a free, open-source companion app for the BT DieselWorks
**AutoSync Manager** website (autosync.btdieselworks.com). It is an independent
project and is not made by or affiliated with BT DieselWorks.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** The developer of this app does not run a server and does not receive,
store, sell or share any of your information. The app contains no analytics, ads or
tracking.

## What the app sends, and where

- **Your AutoSync Manager email and password** are sent only to
  autosync.btdieselworks.com when you sign in, exactly as the website's own sign-in
  form would send them.
- **Setting changes you choose to save** are sent only to autosync.btdieselworks.com,
  the same way the website's Create / Update / Delete buttons send them.
- **Android only – update check:** the app downloads a small version file from this
  project's GitHub Releases page to see whether a newer version exists. No personal
  information is included in that request.

Data you send to autosync.btdieselworks.com is handled under BT DieselWorks' own
policies.

## What is stored on your phone

- The website's sign-in cookie, so you stay signed in. It's removed when you tap
  **Sign out**.
- Your email address (to pre-fill the sign-in screen) and the last vehicle you opened.
- Your **password is never stored**.

All of this stays in the app's private storage on your phone and is deleted if you
uninstall the app.

## Demo mode

**Try demo** uses built-in sample data. Nothing is sent anywhere, and demo changes are
discarded when you exit the demo.

## Children

The app is intended for vehicle owners and is not directed at children.
